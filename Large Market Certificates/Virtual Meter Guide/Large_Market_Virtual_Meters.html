2 · Make these virtual accounts

Fix the NSW LGC factor before linking

The two live accounts use LGCs NSW 24-25 at −0.66 kg/kWh against electricity on 25-26 New South Wales at 0.64, so a 100% meter over-offsets by ~3% and the site goes negative. Victoria has an LGCs Victoria 25-26; NSW needs its 25-26 equivalent added or mapped.

Two rules that decide everything else

Create it empty. Envizi only allows a virtual meter on an account holding no records, so each one is made by hand and the PM&C load tab is never uploaded — it stays as the record of what each account should look like.

Opened On = 2026-07-01. That is what stops the meter reaching back before the renewal. of the sources hold earlier data.

How a site got into section 2

The Site Register's green rows are the renewable ones — 78 of the 81 AU Large Electricity rows. Each row is matched on its NMI (the text after the last underscore in an Envizi account number) to the active account carrying that NMI's electricity from 1 Jul 26; that account becomes the virtual meter's source. Region was never used.

The meter class does not decide scope. Category Management, 4 Sep 26, on a Mogo NMI: "from a metering perspective it may be considered a small site, but from an electricity supply agreement perspective, it's being treated as a large site." So Electricity Small Market in Envizi does not take a site out of the renewal — of the sources carry that style.
